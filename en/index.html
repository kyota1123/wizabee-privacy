日本語

Wizabee ("the App") is an iOS application developed and provided by Kyosuke Tachibana ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

How your information is handled depends on where you live. If you live outside Japan, we do not collect any personal data from you, as described in Section 13. Sections 2 through 8 apply to users in Japan.

1What we never collect

Regardless of where you live, the App never collects any of the following:

  • Location data
  • Audio (the microphone is not used)
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, or other data stored on your device

The only device permission the App requests is access to motion sensors, which it uses to detect your walking cadence. Sensor readings are processed on your device and are used solely to match the music tempo to your steps.

2Information we collect

Only if you live in Japan and turn on collaborator mode as described in Section 14, the App sends the following information to our server. Collaborator mode is off by default, and the App works exactly the same whether or not you turn it on.

This section does not apply to users outside Japan. In those regions no consent prompt is shown and no data is sent. See Section 13.

  • (1) Walking measurements — cadence, step count, duration, and how closely the music stayed in sync
  • (2) Device and app information — device model, app version, iOS version
  • (3) An anonymous ID — an identifier generated automatically by the App. It is not linked to your name or contact details, and we cannot identify you from it.
  • (4) Ratings and comments — only if you choose to submit them. Please do not enter information that could identify you.
  • (5) Technical measurements — playback rate, timing offsets, and configuration values recorded to understand how the App behaved
  • (6) Collaborator mode records — only if you have turned on collaborator mode as described in Section 14

When such data is sent, our server's access log records the originating IP address. This is unavoidable in network communication and is used only to detect unauthorised access and to investigate faults.

3Purpose

We use this information solely to improve step detection and the accuracy with which music is matched to your walking pace.

We do not share or sell it to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4Where information is stored

Information is stored on servers located in Japan (Supabase, Tokyo region).

Collected information is retained for one year in principle and deleted after that period.

5Purchases

Purchases made in the App are processed by Apple Inc. Payment information is handled by Apple Inc. and is never received by us. What we receive from Apple Inc. is aggregated sales figures only.

This section applies wherever you live. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for how Apple Inc. handles your information.

6Turning off data upload

You can stop the upload at any time by turning off collaborator mode (Section 14) under "Contribute" in the Settings tab. Once it is off, all sending described in Section 2 and Section 14 stops, and no further information leaves your device.

Stopping the upload does not delete information that was sent previously. To request deletion, please use the procedure in Section 7.

7Access and deletion requests

To request access to, or deletion of, stored information, please contact us at the address in Section 9, quoting the anonymous ID shown in the Settings tab.

We will normally respond within one month.

Without the anonymous ID we cannot determine which stored records are yours, because we do not hold your name or any other contact details. In that case we are unable to act on the request.

8Security

Communication is encrypted. Access to stored data is restricted, and the App is configured so that one user's device cannot read another user's information.

9Contact

  • Kyosuke Tachibana, developer of Wizabee
  • wizabee.contact@gmail.com

10Children

The App is not directed at children. It should not be used by anyone under 16.

11Changes to this policy

If we change the types of information we collect, we will ask for your consent again within the App.

Other changes take effect when published on this page.

12Automated decision-making

We do not carry out automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

The cadence detection and playback-rate adjustment performed inside the App are device-side features and do not make any determination about your rights or legal status.

13For users outside Japan

13-1 We do not collect personal data

For users outside Japan, we do not collect or process any personal data.

Specifically:

  • No data described in Section 2 is transmitted
  • No consent prompt for data upload is shown
  • No communication with our servers takes place, so no IP address is logged
  • No account is created, no login occurs, and no user is identified

Outside Japan the App operates entirely on your device. Motion sensor readings are processed locally and are never transmitted.

Please note that the privacy information shown on the App Store (the privacy "nutrition label") is a single, worldwide listing. It shows the items collected from users in Japan. This is a consequence of how the listing works and does not contradict the fact that no data is actually collected outside Japan.

13-2 How the region is determined

Data is sent to our server only when the App Store storefront country is Japan and the device's region setting is Japan. If either indicates a region other than Japan, no data is transmitted. If the region cannot be determined, no data is transmitted either.

13-3 We have not appointed an EU representative (for users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland)

Because we do not collect personal data from users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland as described above, we have not appointed a representative in the Union or in the United Kingdom.

For any data protection enquiry, please contact us at the address in Section 9.

13-4 Your rights

As we hold no personal data relating to you, there is no data to which a request for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or objection could apply.

If you contact us, we will confirm this to you. We will normally respond within one month, free of charge.

13-5 Complaints to a supervisory authority (for users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland)

If you have concerns about how we handle your information, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live or work. A list of national supervisory authorities in the EEA is published on the website of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB). In the United Kingdom, the supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

13-6 Purchases

As stated in Section 5, purchases are processed by Apple Inc. For that processing, Apple Inc. acts as a controller independently of us.

13-7 Future changes

If we ever change the App so that we do collect personal data outside Japan, we will revise this policy and put the necessary measures in place — including appointing a representative in the Union where required — before making that change.

14Collaborator mode

14-1 What collaborator mode is

The App includes a way for users to share records from actual use, to help us improve step detection and the accuracy with which music is matched to your steps. We call this collaborator mode.

You can turn on collaborator mode in the Settings tab. It is on only after you agree to the terms of this section. It is off by default. Turning on collaborator mode also turns on the sending of the information described in Section 2.

14-2 What is sent in collaborator mode

While collaborator mode is on, if the music feels wrong and you use your earphones to send a signal, the following information is sent to our server:

  • Motion sensor readings (accelerometer and gyroscope) for a short period before and after the signal
  • The App's internal operating values for the same period, such as playback rate and timing offsets
  • For comparison, one or two periods with no signal from the same session
  • Any note you enter about the signal (optional)
  • Your estimate of how long it took you to send the signal after noticing the problem (chosen from a list)
  • The kind of signal (tempo, sound, or other; you may also leave this unselected)
  • Which song was playing when you sent the signal (for a song included in the App, its identifier; for a song you added yourself, a fixed value meaning "a song you added". The titles of songs you add are never included)
  • The anonymous ID described in Section 2(3), and the version number of the terms you agreed to

The comparison periods are sent because we need a baseline to judge whether the moment you signaled was different from normal.

14-2-2 What is sent when you walk to a song you added

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the terms covering songs you add, the following is sent for each song you played, for walks in which you played songs you added yourself. If you have not agreed to those terms, nothing from that walk is sent at all, including the measurements described in Section 2.

  • The length of the song
  • The tempo of the song, and the multiplier applied to it
  • The file format (whether it is mp3, aac, and so on)
  • Numbers describing how reliably the beat was detected, and the position of the first beat
  • The result of the check made when the song was added (ready to use, or needs a look), and the kind of reason when it needs a look
  • Whether you added a song that was shown as needing a look
  • The result of the quick check made before adding the song, and the kind of reason for it
  • Whether a half-beat correction was applied
  • How the song was added (from the Music app or from the Files app)
  • If you skipped between several songs, the period during which each song played (from how many seconds to how many seconds after you started walking)
  • How the timing offset changed while that song played (a representative value for each of ten equal parts of the time it played)
  • A one-word grouping of the song's character, decided from how evenly the beats are spaced and how far they reach across the song

This is sent so that we can investigate why music does not match your steps for songs you add. The title, the artist name, the file name, and the contents of the file itself are never sent.

14-3 What is not sent

  • From a session in which no signal was sent, no motion sensor readings are sent (only the measurements described in Section 2 are sent)
  • Audio itself is never recorded or sent
  • As stated in Section 1, location data, contacts, photos, and similar data are never collected
  • When you add a song from the Music app, the song is used only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere

14-4 Turning off collaborator mode

You can turn off collaborator mode at any time in the Settings tab. Once it is off, nothing further is sent.

Turning it off does not delete information that was sent previously. To request deletion, please use the procedure in Section 7.

14-5 Where information is stored and for how long

As in Section 4, this information is stored on servers located in Japan, retained for one year in principle, and deleted after that period.

14-6 Users outside Japan

As described in Section 13, collaborator mode is not available to users outside Japan. The collaborator mode item is not shown, and no data is sent.
