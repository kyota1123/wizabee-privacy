日本語

Wizabee ("the App") is an iOS application, including its Apple Watch app, developed and provided by Kyosuke Tachibana ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Effective: June 17, 2026 / Last updated: October 27, 2026

How your information is handled depends on where you live. If you live outside Japan, we do not collect personal data or usage data from you (Section 13 explains this, including the IP address and similar information that briefly reach our server when the App checks the song of the month). Sections 2 through 8 apply to users in Japan. However, the parts of Section 4-2 about the song-of-the-month check and about viewing this policy and the support page apply wherever you live.

1What we never collect

Regardless of where you live, the App never collects any of the following:

  • Audio (the microphone is not used)
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, or other data stored on your device

Location is used only if you choose "GPS with map" in Settings (the map route), and only during regular walks you start in the App (including walks started from Shortcuts or Siri, and excluding "Step detection" (calibration) and "Try it on a walk" / "Try walking" (trial walks)) (Section 18). It is not sent to Wizabee's servers; the map is shown with Apple Maps.

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Motion sensors and motion activity: to detect your walking tempo, to record steps and distance, and to tell walking apart from riding in a vehicle when counting the distance record (Section 17).
  • Media library: requested only when you choose to pick songs from Music.
  • Health (optional): requested only when you turn on "Save walks to Health" or "Heart rate limit" in Settings, and when you press ▶ to start a walk with "Heart rate limit" on (on your iPhone and on your Apple Watch), and when you turn on "Also send heart rate" in the contributor settings (14-2-9). See Section 16.
  • Location (optional): requested ("While Using the App") only when you choose "GPS with map" in Settings. See Section 18.

Sensor readings are processed on your device and are used only to match the music tempo to your steps, to make your walk records, and to count the distance record (Section 17), except the records in 14-2-7 sent in collaborator mode if you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms, and the measurement quality records in 14-2-8, the route info in 14-2-10 (both calculated on your iPhone from sensor readings), and heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit if you turn on the switch in 14-2-9.

Each time you walk, the App saves a record of the walk on your device. This record includes a summary of the walk (date and time, duration, steps, distance, walking tempo, and similar values), values such as your walking tempo every 10 seconds, the time of each step as your foot lands, and values such as your walking tempo and the song's speed about every second. The App also saves on your device the records of your own songs and the distance record (Section 17) and the order of the songs you added that you played most recently. These records are for use within the App and are not sent to us. Only if collaborator mode is on, the walk summary and the step-by-step log (footstrike times, beat times, walking tempo, and playback speed) are sent, and if you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms, the matching summary and Apple Watch button records (14-2-6) and parts of the motion log over Wi-Fi (14-2-7) are also sent, and if you have agreed to the version 2.5 terms, measurement quality records (14-2-8) are also sent (as described in Sections 2 and 14). Unless you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms, version 2.4 or later of the App sends none of the contributor records described in Sections 2 and 14 (14-1; this does not cover the song-of-the-month check in Section 15 or sending your contributor ID for a deletion request in 14-4). The detailed values (every 10 seconds, every step, and about every second) are deleted from your device 90 days after the walk. The walk summary stays. The App installed from the App Store does not record the raw sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values taken 50 times per second), except when it makes the records in 14-2-7 in collaborator mode (deleted once sent). The records in 14-2-7 use these same readings, taken 50 times per second.

The App also saves the following records only on your device and does not send them to us (not even in collaborator mode). From version 2.2, some of them, such as the changes in height, are used for the walk screen (the slope path and graphs). Location is not used. They are deleted when you delete the App (as described in the paragraph below, they may be included in your device backups).

  • A log of changes to the count in the distance record (Section 17) (time, the amount added or used, and the reason, such as a walk, adding a song to the song list, undoing it, or moving over an earlier version's record)
  • Changes in height during each walk (relative height from the start of the walk, derived from air pressure, every few seconds)
  • Floors climbed up and down (from your device's pedometer)
  • Periods of walking, standing still, and running (from your device's motion activity)
  • Times when you switched the tempo mode or used the earphone button (triple press)
  • Times when the heart rate limit took effect (when the rest started and ended, and the total rest time; the heart rate values themselves are not kept). This record is kept in a separate file from your walk records and is excluded from iCloud backup

The App reads these within the "Motion & Fitness" permission you have already given and does not ask for any new permission. If you have not given the permission, or your device lacks the feature, they are not recorded. The App does not keep your average or maximum heart rate (Section 16).

Per-song segments (which song played from when to when, counted from the start of the walk) are kept on this iPhone. In collaborator mode, only the values listed in Section 14 are sent.

Measurement quality records are saved on this iPhone for everyone (for use in the history screens in a later version). In collaborator mode, they are sent as described in 14-2-8. [TO CONFIRM: whether these are saved for people who are not contributors (if not, remove this paragraph), from the v2.1 window's build report]

The records on your device are removed when you delete the App, except for the following:

  • Records included in your iPhone backups (such as iCloud Backup), if you use them. Backups are kept by Apple's systems, and we do not receive them.
  • Workouts saved to Health (Section 16). Delete them in the Health app.
  • Part of the records of your own songs and the distance record may remain in this device's secure storage (the keychain) after you delete the App, and is carried over if you install it again (Section 17).

Media library access is used only to show the list of songs and to copy the songs you pick into the App. Neither the list nor the songs are ever sent off your device.

In addition, wherever you live, the App connects to our sales server to check the song of the month. The App adds only a random one-time value to this connection — none of the information above and no user ID (for the IP address and other details that reach the server by the nature of network communication, see Section 15).

2Information we collect

Only if you live in Japan and turn on collaborator mode as described in Section 14, the App sends the following information to our server. With version 2.4 or later of the App, this also requires that you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms (if you do not agree, none of the records in this section or Section 14 are sent; 14-1). Collaborator mode is off by default, and walking works the same whether or not you turn it on (the thank-you for contributors is described in 14-1).

This section does not apply to users outside Japan. In those regions no consent prompt is shown and none of the information described in this section is sent. See Section 13.

  • (1) Walking measurements — walking tempo, duration, step count, distance (approximate; from the pedometer, not from location), the date and time of the walk, how closely the music stayed in sync, and how long each tempo mode (Normal, Half, Three-quarter) was used and how many times it was switched
  • (2) Device and app information — device model, app version, iOS version
  • (3) Contributor ID (anonymous ID) — a random number created automatically by the App. It is not linked to your name or email address. We cannot learn your name or similar details from this number alone.
  • (4) Ratings — while collaborator mode is on, you can choose a star rating and answers about what didn't match (from given choices) on the summary screen after a walk. They are sent together with that walk's measurements in (1). Notes you write on the summary screen (free text) are never sent, even in collaborator mode; they are saved only on this iPhone. Notes sent in collaborator mode with version 2.1 are deleted one year after we received them and are not used for anything other than the purpose stated at that time (improving step detection and the accuracy with which music is matched to your walking pace).
  • (5) Technical measurements — playback rate, timing offsets, and configuration values recorded to understand how the App behaved
  • (6) Collaborator mode records — only if you have turned on collaborator mode as described in Section 14
  • (7) Step-by-step log — for each step during a walk, the footstrike time, the beat time, your walking tempo, and the playback speed. It is sent after the walk ends (14-2-5).
  • (8) Tempo-matching values — how your walking tempo was read and how playback speed was adjusted (14-2-5).
  • (9) Matching summary and Apple Watch buttons — a summary of how well your steps matched the beat, how you carried your iPhone, the playback-speed range, and presses of the "Out of sync" / "In sync" buttons on Apple Watch (14-2-6).
  • (10) Parts of the motion log over Wi-Fi — iPhone motion-sensor readings from moments such as the start of a walk (14-2-7).
  • (11) Audio delay correction values (the estimated output delay of your earphones or speaker, and any adjustment you made)
  • (12) The end-of-walk question and measurement quality — your answer to the question at the end of walks of 10 minutes or longer (only if you answer), and every 10 seconds, the estimated position and side of your iPhone, missed steps, and how reliably steps were read (14-2-8).
  • (13) Heart rate — only if you turn on "Also send heart rate": the heart rate your Apple Watch measures on walks that use the heart rate limit, and the value and strength of your heart rate limit (14-2-9).
  • (14) Route info — only if you turn on "Also send route info": speed, distance and elevation changes calculated on your iPhone. Latitude and longitude are never sent (14-2-10).

The purpose, storage location, and retention period for sent information are described in Sections 3 and 4.

Even when collaborator mode is on, your heart rate and values read from Health (except heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit if you turn on the switch in 14-2-9), your location (latitude and longitude), the notes on the summary screen, and the distance record are never sent to us. For people who are not contributors, no records about their own songs are sent. For contributors, records about their own songs are limited to the values in 14-2-2 (never the title, the file name or the audio itself). The measurements in this section for that walk (including (11)) are also sent only if you have agreed as described in 14-2-2.

When such data is sent, the originating IP address is recorded in Supabase's access log. We use this log only to detect unauthorised access and to investigate faults. The records in 14-2-7 to 14-2-10 are sent through our receiving program, which runs on Cloudflare Workers, and are stored in Cloudflare R2. This receiving program does not store your IP address, and Cloudflare's request logs (Workers Logs) are turned off for it (your IP address still reaches Cloudflare as part of the connection; Section 4).

3Purpose

We use this information only to improve and develop Wizabee's tempo matching and features such as training. We do not use it for other apps or services.

Results that cannot be linked to any individual (such as control methods, tuned values and statistics) may also be used in our other products and services. This does not include individual records from which only the contributor ID has been removed. Contributors' records themselves are never taken into other apps or services.

On the App Store's privacy information, this use falls under "Analytics".

Apart from entrusting storage and processing to the service providers in Section 4, we do not share or sell this information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4Where information is stored

Information received in collaborator mode is stored in the following places.

  • Supabase (our service provider for storage and processing): a service provided by Supabase Pte. Ltd., a company in Singapore. Its servers are in Japan (Tokyo region).
  • Cloudflare R2 (where the records in 14-2-7 to 14-2-10 are stored): a service provided by Cloudflare, Inc., a company in the United States. We have specified the Asia-Pacific region (Cloudflare places the data there on a best-effort basis, and the exact country can't be specified). As described in the paragraph below, it is kept, in principle, for one year after we receive it.
  • A computer managed by us in Japan:
    • Backup copies for recovery from failures: copies of the records we received, kept for up to 30 days.
    • Working copies for analysis: copies of contributors' records, kept for up to one year after we received them, to improve tempo matching.
    • Both are kept in a location that does not sync with other devices, on an encrypted disk, and are not included in Time Machine or other backups.

Heart rate under 14-2-9 (health-related information) is also stored in Cloudflare R2, like the other records, and is covered by the security measures in Section 8 and our data processing agreement with Cloudflare.

Information we receive is deleted one year after we received it, in principle. Making new copies does not extend this period. Results that can no longer be linked to a contributor ID (such as statistics and values used to tune tempo matching) may be kept after one year.

When we accept a deletion request (Section 7), we delete the data from the server, the backup copies, and the working copies within 30 days of accepting it.

8Security

Communication is encrypted. Access to stored data is restricted, and the App is configured so that one user's device cannot read another user's information.

We also take the following security measures.

  • At our service provider, Supabase, stored information is kept encrypted.
  • Cloudflare R2 encrypts stored objects and their metadata at rest.
  • On our side, only we handle the stored information. The keys for managing it are kept only on our computer (our service providers' staff may handle it as needed under our agreements with them).
  • Our computer's disk is encrypted. Backup copies and working copies of records are kept in a location that does not sync with other devices, and are not included in Time Machine or other backups.
  • We entrust data to our service providers under agreements on how the data is handled (with Supabase, under the Data Processing Addendum that is incorporated into its terms of service; with Cloudflare, under the Data Processing Addendum that forms part of its Main Agreement with us).

For rules in other countries, see Section 4 (4-2).

14Collaborator mode

14-1 What collaborator mode is

The App includes a way for users to share records from actual use, to help us improve step detection and the accuracy with which music is matched to your steps. We call this collaborator mode.

You can turn on collaborator mode under "Contributor" in Settings. It is on only after you agree to the terms of this section. It is off by default. Turning on collaborator mode also turns on the sending of the information described in Section 2. The walking measurements in Section 2 (1) — the record of your walk — include the duration, step count, distance (approximate), and the date and time of the walk.

Contributors receive a benefit. Its details are shown in the app and may change.

While you contribute, you can import your own songs up to the number shown in the app (up to 10 minutes each). What happens to your songs if you stop contributing is shown in the app.

Because version 2.2 sends more (such as the step-by-step log), we ask existing contributors to agree again. If you do not agree, collaborator mode is turned off and the new items are not sent.

Because version 2.4 sends more (the matching summary and Apple Watch buttons in 14-2-6, and parts of the motion log over Wi-Fi in 14-2-7), we ask existing contributors to agree again. If you do not agree, no records are sent (records waiting to be sent are deleted from your iPhone), and your contributor benefits stay the same. Unless you have agreed, version 2.4 or later of the App sends none of the records described in Sections 2 and 14.

In version 2.5, we added your answer to the end-of-walk question and measurement quality records (14-2-8). If you do not agree, we continue to send only the records you agreed to in version 2.4 and do not send the added records. Your contributor benefits stay the same.

If you turn off "Send records" in the contributor settings, we do not send the records in Section 2 and Section 14 for walks finished while it is off. Your contributor benefits stay the same.

If you had collaborator mode on with version 2.1 or earlier: until November 30, 2026 (Japan time), while collaborator mode is on, you can use all five included songs and choose up to six songs you added yourself (you choose which ones). When you turn it off, those choices are released, and just once, right afterwards, you can choose again within the limit that then applies. No song file is ever removed from your device. This is not a purchase, and there is no charge.

If you had collaborator mode on before December 1, 2026 (Japan time), the benefit above applies while you contribute, and after you stop, you keep all the included songs and can use up to six of your own songs (Section 17). This is not a purchase, and there is no charge. No song file is ever removed from your device.

14-2 What is sent in collaborator mode

With version 2.4 or later of the App, each of the records in 14-2-2 to 14-2-10 is sent only if you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms (14-1). The records in 14-2-8 also require that you have agreed to the version 2.5 terms, and the records in 14-2-9 and 14-2-10 are sent only if you turn on the matching switch. Records of walks finished while "Send records" is off are not sent (14-1).

From version 1.7, the App has no way to send a signal, and no new signal records described in this section (the items below) are created. If records created by version 1.6 or earlier have not yet been sent and remain on your device, versions 1.7 to 2.2 of the App may send them, but only while collaborator mode is on and your consent is in place, only after the App confirms that you are using it in Japan, and only within 7 days of their creation (up to 5 records). From version 2.3, the App does not send these records. While collaborator mode is on, records older than 7 days are deleted from the device without being sent. If you turn off collaborator mode, they are not sent.

In version 1.6 or earlier, while collaborator mode is on, if the music feels wrong and you use your earphones to send a signal, the following information is sent to our server:

  • Motion sensor readings (accelerometer and gyroscope) for a short period before and after the signal
  • The App's internal operating values for the same period, such as playback rate and timing offsets
  • For comparison, one or two periods with no signal from the same walk
  • (The fields that earlier versions offered for a note about the signal, your estimate of how long it took you to send it, and the kind of signal had already been removed by version 1.6. None of these are sent.)
  • Which song was playing when you sent the signal (for a song included in the App, its identifier; for a song you added yourself, a fixed value meaning "a song you added". The titles of songs you add are never included)
  • The anonymous ID described in Section 2(3), and the version number of the terms you agreed to

The comparison periods are sent because we need a baseline to judge whether the moment you signaled was different from normal.

14-2-2 What is sent when you walk to a song you added

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the terms covering songs you add, the following is sent for each song you played, for walks in which you played songs you added yourself. If you have not agreed to those terms, nothing from that walk is sent at all, including the measurements described in Section 2.

  • The tempo of the song
  • The multiplier applied to the tempo
  • Numbers describing how reliably the beat was detected
  • The result of the check made when the song was added (ready to use, or needs a look), and the kind of reason when it needs a look
  • The result of the quick check made before adding the song, and the kind of reason for it
  • Whether a half-beat correction was applied
  • How the song was added (from the Music app or from the Files app)
  • If you skipped between several songs, the period during which each song played (from how many seconds to how many seconds after you started walking)
  • A one-word grouping of the song's character, decided from how evenly the beats are spaced and how far they reach across the song
  • From the step-by-step log: the footstrike times and the beat times, the playback speed of the song, and the tempo-matching values (sent under the conditions in 14-2-5)

This is sent so that we can investigate why music does not match your steps for songs you add. The title, the artist name, the file name, and the contents of the file itself are never sent.

14-2-3 App settings

While collaborator mode is on, the following app settings are sent together with the measurements described in Section 2, so that we can learn which settings are used and improve the screens.

  • The screen theme
  • Which rows are shown in the panel below the Play screen (this walk, song, total)
  • Whether tab and panel names are shown in Japanese or English
  • Whether fixed tempo is on or off
  • What happens when you stop walking (pause, stay slow, or back to the song’s tempo)
  • Whether the walking hint has already been shown (not when it was shown)

They are not sent when collaborator mode is off.

14-2-4 Tempo modes

The walking data you share as a collaborator includes how long you used each tempo mode (Normal, Half, Three-quarter) and how many times you switched. We use this to learn how the modes are used and to improve tempo matching. It does not include information that identifies you, such as your name.

  • For each tempo mode (Normal, Half, Three-quarter), how long it was used during a walk (in seconds)
  • How many times the mode was switched with the on-screen button, and how many times with the earphones

Nothing is sent while collaborator mode is off.

14-2-5 Step-by-step log and tempo-matching values (from version 2.2)

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the version 2.2 terms, the following records of a walk are sent after the walk ends. For walks with songs you added, this applies only if you have agreed to the terms in 14-2-2.

  • The footstrike time of each step, and the beat time
  • Your walking tempo and the playback speed
  • How your walking tempo was read and how playback speed was adjusted

These records are stored in Supabase's file storage (Section 4), kept separately for each contributor ID. These records do not include raw motion sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values). They are used to improve tempo matching.

14-2-6 Matching summary and Apple Watch buttons (from version 2.4)

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms, the following records of a walk are sent after the walk ends.

  • A summary of how well your steps matched the beat: the seconds until the matching settled (when you started walking, and each time you set off again after stopping) and how many times this happened, and the number of 10-second windows and the share of them that were matched
  • How you carry your iPhone, as selected in Settings, and the playback-speed range
  • Presses of the "Out of sync" / "In sync" buttons on Apple Watch (how many seconds after recording started, and which button; up to 50 per walk), and the number of presses of each button

We use the records above to improve tempo matching. We do not send the time difference between the Apple Watch and the iPhone. We send heart rate only if you turn on the switch in 14-2-9, and only heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit.

14-2-7 Parts of the motion log over Wi-Fi (from version 2.4)

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the version 2.4 terms, the following records are sent automatically while your iPhone is connected to Wi-Fi.

  • iPhone motion-sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values) from the start of each walk and from 30 seconds around moments such as when the tempo drifted
  • For your first walk as a contributor, and then one walk in every 20, the readings for the whole walk

These readings are kept on your iPhone for up to 3 days after the walk and deleted from your iPhone once sent. Readings not sent within 3 days because there was no Wi-Fi are deleted without being sent. Apple Watch motion values are not sent. These records are used to improve tempo matching. "The start of each walk" means the first 30 seconds after recording starts, and "30 seconds around" means 30 seconds before and 30 seconds after each moment. If the readings for a whole walk are too large, only the start of the walk and the moments are sent instead (and if even those are too large, nothing is sent for that walk).

14-2-8 The end-of-walk question and measurement quality records (from version 2.5)

For contributors who have agreed to the version 2.5 terms, we send:

  • (1) your answer to the question shown at the end of walks of 10 minutes or longer (Easy to walk / Okay / Didn't match), only if you answer; and
  • (2) measurement quality records: every 10 seconds, the estimated position and side of your iPhone, missed steps, and how reliably steps were read.

We use both to improve tempo matching.

14-2-9 Heart rate (from version 2.5, with a switch)

If you turn on "Also send heart rate" and agree on the separate screen, we send, for each walk that uses the heart rate limit, the heart rate values measured by your Apple Watch and how many seconds after the start of the walk each was measured. If you use the heart rate limit, we also send its value and strength (Gentle, Normal or Firm). Heart rate is Health data on your iPhone. We use it only to improve tempo matching and the heart rate limit; we never use it for advertising and never sell it (it is stored as described in Section 4, and no other company uses it for its own purposes). If you turn the switch off, sending stops from your next walk. Like other records, it is generally deleted one year after we receive it (Section 4), and you can ask us to delete it as described in 14-4. On the App Store privacy label, this falls under "Health" (purpose: "Analytics").

14-2-10 Route info (from version 2.5, with a switch)

If you turn on "Also send route info", we send how your speed and distance changed during the walk [TO CONFIRM: how often speed and distance are recorded (every how many seconds), from the v2.1 window's build report], elevation changes (calculated from air pressure), and how each was calculated (steps, air pressure or GPS). All are calculated on your iPhone. We never send latitude and longitude (your location) or the route on the map. GPS is used only if you choose "GPS with map" under "How your route is shown" in Settings, and even then we send only the calculated speed and distance. On the App Store privacy label, this falls under "Fitness".

14-3 What is not sent

  • From a walk in which no signal was sent (including every walk with version 1.7 or later), no raw motion sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values) are sent, except the records in 14-2-7 (the measurements described in Section 2 and the step-by-step log in 14-2-5 are sent; in addition, in App versions up to 2.2, unsent records created by earlier versions may be sent as described in 14-2)
  • Notes on the summary screen (free text) are never sent
  • Audio itself is never recorded or sent
  • As stated in Section 1, contacts, photos, and similar data are never collected. Location for the map route is not sent to Wizabee's servers (Section 18)
  • When you add a song from the Music app, the song is used only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere
  • Your heart rate, values read from Health, your heart rate limit setting, and the records of your own songs (Section 17) and the distance record are never sent, except that, if you turn on the switch in 14-2-9, heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit and the value and strength of your heart rate limit are sent
  • Even when route info is sent, your latitude and longitude (your location) and the route on the map are never sent (14-2-10)

14-4 Turning off collaborator mode

You can stop at any time with Settings > Contributor > Stop contributing. After you stop, no further contributor data is sent (except the contributor ID sent once to request deletion, if you choose "Also delete the data I've sent"). The song-of-the-month check (Section 15) continues regardless of collaborator mode.

If you choose "Also delete the data I've sent" when you stop, we delete the data you've sent within 30 days (Section 7). If you don't, it is deleted, in principle, one year after we receive it, as described in Section 4.

14-5 Where information is stored and for how long

As described in Section 4.

14-6 Users outside Japan

As described in Section 13, collaborator mode is not available to users outside Japan. The collaborator mode item is not shown, and no collaborator mode data is sent.

15Checking the song of the month

15-1 What this connection is for

The App reads, from our sales server (shop-api.wizabee.com), which song is the "song of the month" (it changes on the 1st of each month, Japan time) and the current time. This happens wherever you live and whether or not collaborator mode is on: when you open the App, when you return to it, and when the month changes.

15-2 What is sent

The App sends only a random one-time value, newly created for each request. It is used only to check that the response is genuine, and it does not identify you.

Because of how network communication works, your IP address and the information iOS adds to every request automatically (the App's name and build number, values showing the versions of the iOS networking component and of iOS, and your language settings) also reach the server.

15-3 What is not sent

This connection does not send the anonymous ID, device model, walk records or collaborator mode records described in Section 2, purchase information, the titles or files of songs you added, your location, your name, or similar information.

15-4 What may remain on the server side

Our sales server runs on Cloudflare, Inc.'s platform (Cloudflare Workers). It is configured not to store the content of these requests and not to keep per-request logs (access logs). We do not identify users or create records from this connection.

However, the connection passes through Cloudflare's network (including locations outside Japan). Cloudflare may keep aggregated statistics, such as the number of requests, and records of traffic it judges to be an attack, for a period under its own policies, to operate its service and protect against attacks.

15-5 If the connection fails

Responses carry our signature, and the App uses only content whose signature it has verified. If the App cannot connect, walking works as usual.

16Health and Apple Watch

This section applies wherever you live.

16-1 It is your choice

The Health and Apple Watch features work only when you turn them on in Settings under "Health and Apple Watch" (for contributors, also when you turn on "Also send heart rate" in 14-2-9). They are off by default. When you turn one on, and when you press ▶ to start a walk with "Heart rate limit" on, your iPhone and your Apple Watch each ask for Health permission if you have not yet decided on any of the items. When you turn on "Also send heart rate" in the contributor settings (14-2-9), your iPhone also asks for permission to read heart rate from Health if you have not yet decided; if you do not allow it, the switch turns back off. You can change the permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings. Walking works the same if you leave them off.

16-2 What the App writes to Health

With "Save walks to Health" on, the App saves the duration and distance of each walk to Health as a walking workout. For walks where you measured your heart rate with Wizabee on Apple Watch, the watch saves the workout (including duration, distance, and heart rate), and the iPhone does not save the same walk a second time.

The watch, too, saves the workout only when "Save walks to Health" is on in the App on your iPhone (if the watch could not reach your iPhone during the walk, it follows the setting it last received from your iPhone, and saves nothing if it has never received one). Walks shorter than 60 seconds are not saved by either your iPhone or your Apple Watch, even when the setting is on.

16-3 What the App reads from Health

  • Heart rate: the heart rate measured by Wizabee on Apple Watch during your walks is used to watch your heart rate limit (16-4) (and, for contributors who turn on the switch in 14-2-9, to improve tempo matching and the heart rate limit). The App does not keep your heart rate on your iPhone after a walk [TO CONFIRM: whether heart rate is kept on the iPhone until it is sent under 14-2-9 (if so, state that it is deleted once sent and for how many days it is kept), from the v2.1 window's build report]. In collaborator mode, heart rate is sent only if you turn on the switch in 14-2-9, and only heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit.
  • Workouts: the App reads workouts that Apple Watch or other apps saved for the same time, so that it does not save the same walk twice.

16-4 Heart rate limit

When you turn on "Heart rate limit" and enter a limit, Wizabee on Apple Watch opens when you start walking and measures your heart rate. Your heart rate goes directly from your watch to your iPhone through Apple's workout system. If it stays above your limit, the App gradually slows the tempo of the music, and returns it when your heart rate comes down.

The limit is a number you choose as a guide. The App is not a medical device. It does not assess your health or give any diagnosis or treatment advice.

16-5 Not sent, not used for anything else

Your heart rate and the values read from Health are used only on your iPhone and Apple Watch and are never sent to our server, except heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit, for contributors who turn on the switch in 14-2-9. We do not give them to third parties, use them for advertising, or sell them. We do not store them in iCloud. Of the records about Apple Watch, the only ones sent in collaborator mode are button presses (14-2-6) and, if you turn on the switch in 14-2-9, heart rate measured on walks that use the heart rate limit.

16-6 Where they remain and how to delete them

  • The App does not keep heart rate values in its records on your iPhone [TO CONFIRM: same as the mark in 16-3].
  • Workouts saved to Health remain in Health after you delete the App. Delete them in the Health app.
  • When a workout is not saved (the setting is off, or the walk was shorter than 60 seconds), Wizabee on Apple Watch deletes the values it wrote to Health itself during that walk (such as distance and heart rate). It never deletes values recorded by other apps or devices (including Apple Watch itself).

17Records of your own songs, and the distance record

This section applies wherever you live.

17-1 How many of your own songs you can use, and locked songs

Without Wizabee Plus, you can use up to 5 songs you added yourself (each up to 10 minutes). The song of the month and songs you bought don't count. With Wizabee Plus, there is no limit on the number of songs, and each can be up to 3 hours long. When Wizabee Plus ends, your 5 most-played songs stay available, and your other songs are locked for safekeeping instead of being deleted. You can swap a locked song with one of the songs you kept, and if you subscribe to Wizabee Plus again, you can use them all. For the App obtained from the App Store, these rules apply from December 1, 2026 (Japan time). For collaborator mode, see 14-1.

17-2 How "most-played" is worked out

The most-played order is worked out on your device from the song segments in your walk records (Section 1), by adding up how long each song played (if two songs are equal, the one you played more recently comes first). Location is not used.

17-3 The distance record (a feature of earlier versions)

Earlier versions had a count that grew with the distance you walked while music was playing, and a way to use that count to add songs to a song list. From version 2.1, this feature is no longer shown in the App and cannot be used. However, the App keeps making its record of that count and the distance counted, on your device, in the same way as earlier versions. The distance is counted on your device, only for the parts of a walk where the pedometer distance, the App's step detection, and Apple's motion activity (walking or in a vehicle) agree, and music was playing. Location is not used. The count cannot be bought with money and has no cash value.

17-4 Kept only on your device

Which of your songs are available and which are locked (identified by a value made from the file's content, not by title), the count in 17-3, the distance counted for each day, and the order of the songs you added that you played most recently are stored only on your device and are never sent to us, even when collaborator mode is on.

17-5 New phones and deletion

Because the records of your own songs and the distance record exist only on your device, they move to a new iPhone only when you restore it from an encrypted backup (including iCloud Backup). They are not synced through iCloud Keychain. We do not hold these records, so we cannot restore them for you.

18Map route (optional)

This section applies wherever you live.

18-1 Your choice

Only if you choose "GPS with map" under "How your route is shown" in Settings does the App ask for location permission ("While Using the App"). If you allow it, the App draws your walking route on a map (called the "map route" in this section). It is not chosen at first; the App then shows only the slope path, which uses no location. The App never asks for "Always".

18-2 When it records

The App records your location every 5 seconds, only during regular walks you start in the App (including walks started from Shortcuts or Siri). It does not record during "Step detection" (calibration) or "Try it on a walk" / "Try walking" (trial walks). If you turn off the screen and put your iPhone in your pocket, it keeps recording during that walk (a blue indicator appears at the top of the screen meanwhile). It does not record when you are not on a walk.

18-3 What is not sent

Your location is used only to draw the route on the walk screen and is not sent to Wizabee's servers, not even when collaborator mode is on. To show the map, the App loads map data for the area being shown from Apple. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. Exported or shared records do not include the route.

18-4 Where it is kept and how to delete it

The route is saved only on this iPhone and is not included in iCloud Backup. If you delete the Wizabee app, the route is deleted too. The first and last 200 m of the route are hidden at first.
