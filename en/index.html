日本語

Wizabee ("the App") is an iOS application, including its Apple Watch app, developed and provided by Kyosuke Tachibana ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Effective: June 17, 2026 / Last updated: October 19, 2026

How your information is handled depends on where you live. If you live outside Japan, we do not collect personal data or usage data from you (Section 13 explains this, including the IP address and similar information that briefly reach our server when the App checks the song of the month). Sections 2 through 8 apply to users in Japan. However, the parts of Section 4-2 about the song-of-the-month check and about viewing this policy and the support page apply wherever you live.

1What we never collect

Regardless of where you live, the App never collects any of the following:

  • Location data
  • Audio (the microphone is not used)
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, or other data stored on your device

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Motion sensors and motion activity: to detect your walking tempo, to record steps and distance, and to tell walking apart from riding in a vehicle when counting the distance record (Section 17).
  • Media library: requested only when you choose to pick songs from Music.
  • Health (optional): requested only when you turn on "Save walks to Health" or "Heart rate limit" in Settings, and when you press ▶ to start a walk with "Heart rate limit" on (on your iPhone and on your Apple Watch). See Section 16.

Sensor readings are processed on your device and are used only to match the music tempo to your steps, to make your walk records, and to count the distance record (Section 17).

Each time you walk, the App saves a record of the walk on your device. This record includes a summary of the walk (date and time, duration, steps, distance, walking tempo, and similar values), values such as your walking tempo every 10 seconds, the time of each step as your foot lands, and values such as your walking tempo and the song's speed about every second. The App also saves on your device the records of your own songs and the distance record (Section 17) and the order of the songs you added that you played most recently. These records are for use within the App and are not sent to us. Only if collaborator mode is on, the walk summary and the step-by-step log (footstrike times, beat times, walking tempo, and playback speed) are sent (as described in Sections 2 and 14). The detailed values (every 10 seconds, every step, and about every second) are deleted from your device 90 days after the walk. The walk summary stays. The App installed from the App Store does not record the raw sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values taken 50 times per second).

The App also saves the following records only on your device and does not send them to us (not even in collaborator mode). From version 2.2, some of them, such as the changes in height, are used for the walk screen (the slope path and graphs). Location is not used. They are deleted when you delete the App (as described in the paragraph below, they may be included in your device backups).

  • A log of changes to the count in the distance record (Section 17) (time, the amount added or used, and the reason, such as a walk, adding a song to the song list, undoing it, or moving over an earlier version's record)
  • Changes in height during each walk (relative height from the start of the walk, derived from air pressure, every few seconds)
  • Floors climbed up and down (from your device's pedometer)
  • Periods of walking, standing still, and running (from your device's motion activity)
  • Periods for each song (the song, start and end times, and your average walking tempo and the song's average speed during that period)
  • Times when you switched the tempo mode or used the earphone button (triple press)
  • Times when the heart rate limit took effect (when the rest started and ended, and the total rest time; the heart rate values themselves are not kept). This record is kept in a separate file from your walk records and is excluded from iCloud backup

The App reads these within the "Motion & Fitness" permission you have already given and does not ask for any new permission. If you have not given the permission, or your device lacks the feature, they are not recorded. The App does not keep your average or maximum heart rate (Section 16).

The records on your device are removed when you delete the App, except for the following:

  • Records included in your iPhone backups (such as iCloud Backup), if you use them. Backups are kept by Apple's systems, and we do not receive them.
  • Workouts saved to Health (Section 16). Delete them in the Health app.
  • Part of the records of your own songs and the distance record may remain in this device's secure storage (the keychain) after you delete the App, and is carried over if you install it again (Section 17).

Media library access is used only to show the list of songs and to copy the songs you pick into the App. Neither the list nor the songs are ever sent off your device.

In addition, wherever you live, the App connects to our sales server to check the song of the month. The App adds only a random one-time value to this connection — none of the information above and no user ID (for the IP address and other details that reach the server by the nature of network communication, see Section 15).

2Information we collect

Only if you live in Japan and turn on collaborator mode as described in Section 14, the App sends the following information to our server. Collaborator mode is off by default, and walking works the same whether or not you turn it on (the thank-you for contributors is described in 14-1).

This section does not apply to users outside Japan. In those regions no consent prompt is shown and none of the information described in this section is sent. See Section 13.

  • (1) Walking measurements — walking tempo, duration, step count, distance (approximate; from the pedometer, not from location), the date and time of the walk, how closely the music stayed in sync, and how long each tempo mode (Normal, Half, Three-quarter) was used and how many times it was switched
  • (2) Device and app information — device model, app version, iOS version
  • (3) Contributor ID (anonymous ID) — a random number created automatically by the App. It is not linked to your name or email address. We cannot learn your name or similar details from this number alone.
  • (4) Ratings — while collaborator mode is on, you can choose a star rating and answers about what didn't match (from given choices) on the summary screen after a walk. They are sent together with that walk's measurements in (1). Notes you write on the summary screen (free text) are never sent, even in collaborator mode; they are saved only on this iPhone. Notes sent in collaborator mode with version 2.1 are deleted one year after we received them and are not used for anything other than the purpose stated at that time (improving step detection and the accuracy with which music is matched to your walking pace).
  • (5) Technical measurements — playback rate, timing offsets, and configuration values recorded to understand how the App behaved
  • (6) Collaborator mode records — only if you have turned on collaborator mode as described in Section 14
  • (7) Step-by-step log — for each step during a walk, the footstrike time, the beat time, your walking tempo, and the playback speed. It is sent after the walk ends (14-2-5).
  • (8) Tempo-matching values — how your walking tempo was read and how playback speed was adjusted (14-2-5).

The purpose, storage location, and retention period for sent information are described in Sections 3 and 4.

Even when collaborator mode is on, your heart rate, values read from Health, your location, the notes on the summary screen, and the records of your own songs and the distance record are never sent.

When such data is sent, the originating IP address is recorded in Supabase's access log. We use this log only to detect unauthorised access and to investigate faults.

3Purpose

We use this information only to improve and develop Wizabee's tempo matching and features such as training. We do not use it for other apps or services.

Results that cannot be linked to any individual (such as control methods, tuned values and statistics) may also be used in our other products and services. This does not include individual records from which only the contributor ID has been removed. Contributors' records themselves are never taken into other apps or services.

On the App Store's privacy information, this use falls under "Analytics".

Apart from entrusting storage and processing to the service providers in Section 4, we do not share or sell this information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4Where information is stored

Information received in collaborator mode is stored in the following places.

  • Supabase (our service provider for storage and processing): a service provided by Supabase Pte. Ltd., a company in Singapore. Its servers are in Japan (Tokyo region).
  • A computer managed by us in Japan:
    • Backup copies for recovery from failures: copies of the records we received, kept for up to 30 days.
    • Working copies for analysis: copies of contributors' records, kept for up to one year after we received them, to improve tempo matching.
    • Both are kept in a location that does not sync with other devices, on an encrypted disk, and are not included in Time Machine or other backups.

Information we receive is deleted one year after we received it, in principle. Making new copies does not extend this period. Results that can no longer be linked to a contributor ID (such as statistics and values used to tune tempo matching) may be kept after one year.

When we accept a deletion request (Section 7), we delete the data from the server, the backup copies, and the working copies within 30 days of accepting it.

For access under that Code to personal information held by businesses, there are said to be no provisions on approval by an independent body, on limits on the handling and security of the information obtained, on transparency, or on oversight, investigation and review mechanisms to ensure compliance with the law. 14Collaborator mode

14-1 What collaborator mode is

The App includes a way for users to share records from actual use, to help us improve step detection and the accuracy with which music is matched to your steps. We call this collaborator mode.

You can turn on collaborator mode under "Contributor" in Settings. It is on only after you agree to the terms of this section. It is off by default. Turning on collaborator mode also turns on the sending of the information described in Section 2. The walking measurements in Section 2 (1) — the record of your walk — include the duration, step count, distance (approximate), and the date and time of the walk.

If you become a contributor with version 2.2 or later, you can import your own songs with no limit on the number while you contribute (up to 10 minutes each). This is a limited campaign and may change. If you stop, your 5 most-played songs stay, and the rest are kept locked instead of being deleted. This is not a purchase, there is no charge, and it is not a substitute for buying Wizabee Plus.

Because version 2.2 sends more (such as the step-by-step log), we ask existing contributors to agree again. If you do not agree, collaborator mode is turned off and the new items are not sent.

If you had collaborator mode on with version 2.1 or earlier: until November 30, 2026 (Japan time), while collaborator mode is on, you can use all five included songs and choose up to six songs you added yourself (you choose which ones). When you turn it off, those choices are released, and just once, right afterwards, you can choose again within the limit that then applies. No song file is ever removed from your device. This is not a purchase, and there is no charge.

If you had collaborator mode on before December 1, 2026 (Japan time), the version 2.2 terms above (no limit on the number, up to 10 minutes each) apply while you contribute, and after you stop, you keep all the included songs and can use up to six of your own songs (Section 17). This is not a purchase, and there is no charge. No song file is ever removed from your device.

14-2 What is sent in collaborator mode

From version 1.7, the App has no way to send a signal, and no new information described in this section is created. However, if records created by version 1.6 or earlier have not yet been sent and remain on your device, they may be sent from version 1.7 or later, but only while Collaborator Mode is on and your consent is in place, and only within 7 days of their creation (up to 5 records). Records are sent only if Collaborator Mode is on and you have given consent, and only after the App confirms that you are using it in Japan. Records older than 7 days are deleted from the device without being sent. If you turn off Collaborator Mode, they are not sent.

In version 1.6 or earlier, while collaborator mode is on, if the music feels wrong and you use your earphones to send a signal, the following information is sent to our server:

  • Motion sensor readings (accelerometer and gyroscope) for a short period before and after the signal
  • The App's internal operating values for the same period, such as playback rate and timing offsets
  • For comparison, one or two periods with no signal from the same walk
  • (The fields that earlier versions offered for a note about the signal, your estimate of how long it took you to send it, and the kind of signal had already been removed by version 1.6. None of these are sent.)
  • Which song was playing when you sent the signal (for a song included in the App, its identifier; for a song you added yourself, a fixed value meaning "a song you added". The titles of songs you add are never included)
  • The anonymous ID described in Section 2(3), and the version number of the terms you agreed to

The comparison periods are sent because we need a baseline to judge whether the moment you signaled was different from normal.

14-2-2 What is sent when you walk to a song you added

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the terms covering songs you add, the following is sent for each song you played, for walks in which you played songs you added yourself. If you have not agreed to those terms, nothing from that walk is sent at all, including the measurements described in Section 2.

  • The length of the song
  • The tempo of the song, and the multiplier applied to it
  • The file format (whether it is mp3, aac, and so on)
  • Numbers describing how reliably the beat was detected, and the position of the first beat
  • The result of the check made when the song was added (ready to use, or needs a look), and the kind of reason when it needs a look
  • Whether you added a song that was shown as needing a look
  • The result of the quick check made before adding the song, and the kind of reason for it
  • Whether a half-beat correction was applied
  • How the song was added (from the Music app or from the Files app)
  • If you skipped between several songs, the period during which each song played (from how many seconds to how many seconds after you started walking)
  • How the timing offset changed while that song played (a representative value for each of ten equal parts of the time it played)
  • A one-word grouping of the song's character, decided from how evenly the beats are spaced and how far they reach across the song

This is sent so that we can investigate why music does not match your steps for songs you add. The title, the artist name, the file name, and the contents of the file itself are never sent.

14-2-3 App settings

While Collaborator Mode is on, the following app settings are sent together with the measurements described in Section 2, so that we can learn which settings are used and improve the screens.

  • The screen theme
  • Which rows are shown in the panel below the Play screen (this walk, song, total)
  • Whether tab and panel names are shown in Japanese or English
  • Whether fixed tempo is on or off
  • What happens when you stop walking (pause, stay slow, or back to the song’s tempo)
  • Whether the walking hint has already been shown (not when it was shown)

They are not sent when Collaborator Mode is off.

14-2-4 Tempo modes

The walking data you share as a collaborator includes how long you used each tempo mode (Normal, Half, Three-quarter) and how many times you switched. We use this to learn how the modes are used and to improve tempo matching. It does not include information that identifies you, such as your name.

  • For each tempo mode (Normal, Half, Three-quarter), how long it was used during a walk (in seconds)
  • How many times the mode was switched with the on-screen button, and how many times with the earphones

Nothing is sent while Collaborator Mode is off.

14-2-5 Step-by-step log and tempo-matching values (from version 2.2)

Only while collaborator mode is on and you have agreed to the version 2.2 terms, the following records of a walk are sent after the walk ends. For walks with songs you added, this applies only if you have agreed to the terms in 14-2-2.

  • The footstrike time of each step, and the beat time
  • Your walking tempo and the playback speed
  • How your walking tempo was read and how playback speed was adjusted

These records are stored in Supabase's file storage (Section 4), kept separately for each contributor ID. They do not include raw motion sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values). They are used to improve tempo matching.

14-3 What is not sent

  • From a walk in which no signal was sent (including every walk with version 1.7 or later), no raw motion sensor readings (acceleration and rotation values) are sent (the measurements described in Section 2 and the step-by-step log in 14-2-5 are sent; this excludes unsent records created by earlier versions, as described in 14-2)
  • Notes on the summary screen (free text) are never sent
  • Audio itself is never recorded or sent
  • As stated in Section 1, location data, contacts, photos, and similar data are never collected
  • When you add a song from the Music app, the song is used only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere
  • Your heart rate, values read from Health, your heart rate limit setting, and the records of your own songs and the distance record are never sent

14-4 Turning off collaborator mode

You can stop at any time with Settings > Contributor > Stop contributing. After you stop, no further contributor data is sent. The song-of-the-month check (Section 15) continues regardless of collaborator mode.

The data you've sent is deleted after one year.

14-5 Where information is stored and for how long

As described in Section 4.

14-6 Users outside Japan

As described in Section 13, collaborator mode is not available to users outside Japan. The collaborator mode item is not shown, and no collaborator mode data is sent.

15Checking the song of the month

15-1 What this connection is for

The App reads, from our sales server (shop-api.wizabee.com), which song is the "song of the month" (it changes on the 1st of each month, Japan time) and the current time. This happens wherever you live and whether or not collaborator mode is on: when you open the App, when you return to it, and when the month changes.

15-2 What is sent

The App sends only a random one-time value, newly created for each request. It is used only to check that the response is genuine, and it does not identify you.

Because of how network communication works, your IP address and the information iOS adds to every request automatically (the App's name and build number, values showing the versions of the iOS networking component and of iOS, and your language settings) also reach the server.

15-3 What is not sent

This connection does not send the anonymous ID, device model, walk records or collaborator mode records described in Section 2, purchase information, the titles or files of songs you added, your location, your name, or similar information.

15-4 What may remain on the server side

Our sales server runs on Cloudflare, Inc.'s platform (Cloudflare Workers). It is configured not to store the content of these requests and not to keep per-request logs (access logs). We do not identify users or create records from this connection.

However, the connection passes through Cloudflare's network (including locations outside Japan). Cloudflare may keep aggregated statistics, such as the number of requests, and records of traffic it judges to be an attack, for a period under its own policies, to operate its service and protect against attacks.

15-5 If the connection fails

Responses carry our signature, and the App uses only content whose signature it has verified. If the App cannot connect, walking works as usual.

16Health and Apple Watch

This section applies wherever you live.

16-1 It is your choice

The Health and Apple Watch features work only when you turn them on in Settings under "Health and Apple Watch". They are off by default. When you turn one on, and when you press ▶ to start a walk with "Heart rate limit" on, your iPhone and your Apple Watch each ask for Health permission if you have not yet decided on any of the items. You can change the permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings. Walking works the same if you leave them off.

16-2 What the App writes to Health

With "Save walks to Health" on, the App saves the duration and distance of each walk to Health as a walking workout. For walks where you measured your heart rate with Wizabee on Apple Watch, the watch saves the workout (including duration, distance, and heart rate), and the iPhone does not save the same walk a second time.

The watch, too, saves the workout only when "Save walks to Health" is on in the App on your iPhone (if the watch could not reach your iPhone during the walk, it follows the setting it last received from your iPhone, and saves nothing if it has never received one). Walks shorter than 60 seconds are not saved by either your iPhone or your Apple Watch, even when the setting is on.

16-3 What the App reads from Health

  • Heart rate: the heart rate measured by Wizabee on Apple Watch during your walks is used only to watch your heart rate limit (16-4). The App does not keep your heart rate on your iPhone after a walk.
  • Workouts: the App reads workouts that Apple Watch or other apps saved for the same time, so that it does not save the same walk twice.

16-4 Heart rate limit

When you turn on "Heart rate limit" and enter a limit, Wizabee on Apple Watch opens when you start walking and measures your heart rate. Your heart rate goes directly from your watch to your iPhone through Apple's workout system. If it stays above your limit, the App gradually slows the tempo of the music, and returns it when your heart rate comes down.

The limit is a number you choose as a guide. The App is not a medical device. It does not assess your health or give any diagnosis or treatment advice.

16-5 Not sent, not used for anything else

Your heart rate and the values read from Health are used only on your iPhone and Apple Watch and are never sent to our server, even when collaborator mode is on. We do not give them to third parties, use them for advertising, or sell them. We do not store them in iCloud.

16-6 Where they remain and how to delete them

  • The App does not keep heart rate values in its records on your iPhone.
  • Workouts saved to Health remain in Health after you delete the App. Delete them in the Health app.
  • When a workout is not saved (the setting is off, or the walk was shorter than 60 seconds), Wizabee on Apple Watch deletes the values it wrote to Health itself during that walk (such as distance and heart rate). It never deletes values recorded by other apps or devices (including Apple Watch itself).

17Records of your own songs, and the distance record

This section applies wherever you live.

17-1 How many of your own songs you can use, and locked songs

Without Wizabee Plus, you can use up to 5 songs you added yourself (each up to 10 minutes). The song of the month and songs you bought don't count. With Wizabee Plus, there is no limit on the number of songs, and each can be up to 3 hours long. When Wizabee Plus ends, your 5 most-played songs stay available, and your other songs are locked for safekeeping instead of being deleted. You can swap a locked song with one of the songs you kept, and if you subscribe to Wizabee Plus again, you can use them all. For the App obtained from the App Store, these rules apply from December 1, 2026 (Japan time). For collaborator mode, see 14-1.

17-2 How "most-played" is worked out

The most-played order is worked out on your device from the song segments in your walk records (Section 1), by adding up how long each song played (if two songs are equal, the one you played more recently comes first). Location is not used.

17-3 The distance record (a feature of earlier versions)

Earlier versions had a count that grew with the distance you walked while music was playing, and a way to use that count to add songs to a song list. From version 2.1, this feature is no longer shown in the App and cannot be used. However, the App keeps making its record of that count and the distance counted, on your device, in the same way as earlier versions. The distance is counted on your device, only for the parts of a walk where the pedometer distance, the App's step detection, and Apple's motion activity (walking or in a vehicle) agree, and music was playing. Location is not used. The count cannot be bought with money and has no cash value.

17-4 Kept only on your device

Which of your songs are available and which are locked (identified by a value made from the file's content, not by title), the count in 17-3, the distance counted for each day, and the order of the songs you added that you played most recently are stored only on your device and are never sent to us, even when collaborator mode is on.

17-5 New phones and deletion

Because the records of your own songs and the distance record exist only on your device, they move to a new iPhone only when you restore it from an encrypted backup (including iCloud Backup). They are not synced through iCloud Keychain. We do not hold these records, so we cannot restore them for you.
