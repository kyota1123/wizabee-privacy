Wizabee SupportiOS app

Wizabee is an iPhone app that matches the tempo of your music to your walking pace.

1Contact

For questions, bug reports, or data deletion requests, please email wizabee.contact@gmail.com.

2Frequently asked questions

Q. The tempo does not match my pace.

A. First, in Settings > Walking feel, set Phone position to where you carry your iPhone, and run Step detection. If it still feels off after a walk, adjust it in Walking feel > How the sound lines up.

Q. The tempo does not change at all when I walk.

A. Check that Wizabee is allowed under Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness on your iPhone. Detection settles faster if you walk a few steps outdoors or march in place.

Q. It feels out of sync with Bluetooth earphones.

A. Bluetooth audio latency varies considerably between models, which can make the sync feel off. Try playing through the iPhone's built-in speaker.

Q. Why does the volume change by itself?

A. It may be one of these:

  • Wizabee automatically matches the loudness of your own songs to the included songs. Songs you added earlier are adjusted in the background when you are not walking, so until then they play as before. This leveling never changes the volume in the middle of a song.
  • If Personalized Volume or Conversation Awareness is on for your AirPods, the volume changes with the sounds and voices around you. You can turn them off by tapping your AirPods' name in iPhone Settings.
  • The iPhone's volume buttons may be pressed in a pouch or pocket.

Q. What does Wizabee save to Health, and how do I delete it?

A. This is off by default. Wizabee saves to Health only when you turn on "Save walks to Health" in Settings under "Health and Apple Watch". For walks with just your iPhone, it saves the duration and distance as a walking workout. For walks where you measured your heart rate with Wizabee on Apple Watch, the watch saves the workout (including duration, distance, and heart rate).

Saved workouts remain in Health even if you delete Wizabee. To delete them, use the Health app. If you use Apple's Workout app at the same time, the same walk may be saved twice. If that happens, delete the one you don't need in the Health app.

Q. The music slows down when I run fast.

A. When you run faster than about 200 steps per minute, Wizabee may read your foot tempo as half of your actual pace, and the music may play slowly. This is a known behavior in the current version, and we are working to improve it.

3Privacy

Outside Japan, Wizabee sends no personal data or usage data to the developer. The only connection to the developer's server is the check for the song of the month, which sends just a random one-time value. Your IP address reaches the server only as part of that connection; it is not stored or used to identify you. Your walks, your songs, and your heart rate stay on your devices. See the Privacy Policy for details.
