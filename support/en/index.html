Wizabee SupportiOS app

Wizabee is an iPhone app that matches the tempo of your music to your walking pace.

1Contact

For questions, bug reports, or data deletion requests, please email wizabee.contact@gmail.com.

2Frequently asked questions

Q. The tempo does not match my pace.

A. Use Calibration in Settings to adjust for your device and how you carry it. Detection settles faster if you walk a few steps outdoors or march in place.

Q. The tempo does not change at all when I walk.

A. Check that Wizabee is allowed under Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness on your iPhone. Detection settles faster if you walk a few steps outdoors or march in place.

Q. It feels out of sync with Bluetooth earphones.

A. Bluetooth audio latency varies considerably between models, which can make the sync feel off. Try playing through the iPhone's built-in speaker.

3Privacy

Outside Japan, Wizabee sends no data to the developer's servers. Everything runs on your device.
